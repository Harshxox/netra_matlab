PREVENTABLE VISION LOSS

Why Routine Retinal Screening Matters

Diabetic Retinopathy develops silently with zero pain or early symptoms. Netra acts as a frontline triage screening system to catch microaneurysms before vision loss becomes irreversible.

️
90%+ Preventable

Over 90% of severe vision loss can be prevented through routine annual dilated screenings and timely medical referral.
